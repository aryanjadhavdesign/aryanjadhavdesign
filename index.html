02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

A little
about me.

Curiosity first. Craft always. A work in progress, by design.

Think.

Hey, I'm Aryan Mithil Jadhav, an Applied Arts student at J.K. Art College, Wadala. I enjoy turning ideas into visual stories — whether that's a bold poster, a character with personality, a brand identity or an interface people can use.

My work moves between hand-drawn and ink-based illustration, anime-inspired art, graphic communication and digital design. I'm learning, experimenting and looking for opportunities to make thoughtful work with good people.

03 / HOW I APPROACH IT

From thought
to form.

A flexible process that keeps the idea at the centre and the details working hard.

01 / OBSERVE

Find the spark.

Understand the brief, collect references and look for the idea worth pursuing.

02 / EXPLORE

Make a mess.

Sketch, experiment with type, test compositions and follow unexpected directions.

03 / REFINE

Make it click.

Build hierarchy, sharpen the visual language and make every element earn its place.

04 / DELIVER

Put it out there.

Prepare the final artwork, presentation and formats for the real world.
